Leave Policy
Employees get 24 paid leaves per year. Unused leaves up to 10 can be carried forward. Sick leave needs a doctor's note after 2 days.

Work From Home
Engineers may work from home 3 days a week. Fully remote work needs manager and HR approval.

Laptops and Equipment
Every new joiner gets a MacBook Pro. Lost laptops must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

Payroll
Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. Reimbursements are paid with the next salary.

Travel
Domestic flights must be booked through the travel portal. International travel needs VP approval.The company is based in India; travel within India counts as domestic.
